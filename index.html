About Me

I am a PhD researcher specializing in Computer Vision. My research focuses on developing machine learning models that can generalize reliably beyond the domains and distributions observed during training.

My current interests include Domain Generalization, Source-Free Domain Generalization, robust visual representation learning, and vision-language models. I am particularly interested in understanding why current models fail under distribution shifts and designing methods that improve their robustness.

Research

My research explores how visual models can remain reliable when deployed on data distributions that differ from those seen during training.

01

Domain Generalization

Learning representations that transfer effectively to unseen domains without requiring access to target domain data during training.

02

Source-Free Generalization

Exploring generalization when source training images are unavailable and only learned knowledge or semantic information can be used.

03

Robust Representation Learning

Developing learning strategies that encourage invariant and transferable visual representations under distribution shifts.
